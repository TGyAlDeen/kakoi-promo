Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 14 May 2026

This page explains how to delete the data Kakoi holds about you, including any data we received from Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Threads) when you connected those accounts.

In short: You can delete everything in three ways — disconnect a single platform, delete your full Kakoi account from inside the app, or email us if you can no longer access the app. We complete all deletion requests within 30 days, often much sooner.

Option A — Disconnect a single platform

If you only want to revoke Kakoi's access to one specific Meta account (e.g., disconnect Instagram while keeping Facebook connected), the fastest way is from inside the app:

  1. Sign in to Kakoi at kakoi.app.
  2. Go to Settings → Platforms.
  3. Click Disconnect next to the account you want to remove.
  4. Confirm the action. We immediately revoke our stored access token for that account, then delete the following within 30 days:
    • The access token (deleted from our database)
    • Cached posts, comments, and insights we received from that platform
    • Card images and other artifacts generated specifically for that platform

Disconnecting one platform does not delete your Kakoi account or your data on other connected platforms.

Option B — Delete your entire Kakoi account

If you want to remove everything — your account, every connected platform, all generated content, all analytics caches — use the in-app deletion flow:

  1. Sign in to Kakoi.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Scroll to "Delete account" at the bottom of the page.
  4. Click Delete my account and type your account name to confirm.
  5. You will be signed out immediately.

What happens after you delete:

  • Immediately: your session ends, all platform access tokens are revoked, the account is marked for deletion and cannot be recovered.
  • Within 7 days: all personal data, content, and platform data is purged from active systems.
  • Within 30 days: data is removed from encrypted backups (which rotate on a weekly cycle).
  • Compliance log: we keep a minimal deletion-record (account ID, deletion timestamp) for up to 1 year solely to demonstrate compliance with deletion requests. This record contains no personal information beyond the internal user ID.
This action is irreversible. Once deletion is initiated, we cannot restore your account, your published posts, your analytics history, or any saved drafts. If you want to keep a copy, use Settings → Account → Export first.

Option C — Email request (if you cannot access the app)

If you've lost access to the Meta account you used to sign in to Kakoi, or you cannot reach the in-app deletion flow for any reason, send a deletion request to:

  • Email: [PRIVACY_EMAIL — replace before publishing, e.g., privacy@kakoi.app]

Include the following information so we can identify your account:

  • The email address you used at Kakoi (if you remember it)
  • The username of the Instagram or Facebook account you connected
  • The approximate date you signed up
  • A clear statement that you want your account deleted

We respond to email deletion requests within 3 business days to confirm receipt and verify your identity. Verified requests are completed within 30 days.

Removing Kakoi from your Meta account

Even after Kakoi deletes its copy of your data, you may also want to revoke Kakoi's app access directly from your Meta settings, as an extra step:

Facebook

  1. Open Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.
  2. Find Kakoi in the list of "Active" apps.
  3. Click Remove.

Instagram

  1. Open Instagram → Settings → Security → Apps and Websites → Active.
  2. Find Kakoi and tap Remove.

Once revoked at the Meta level, even if we somehow held a copy of your token (we do not — see above), it can no longer be used.

What we keep, and why

Even after a deletion request, we retain a minimal set of records as required for compliance, fraud prevention, or legal purposes:

  • Deletion record: internal user ID + deletion timestamp. Retained 1 year. Contains no personal information.
  • Aggregated, anonymized analytics: high-level numbers like "Kakoi processed X posts in May 2026" that cannot be linked back to any individual.
  • Billing records: where applicable, invoices and payment records are retained as required by applicable tax law (typically 7 years), but redacted to remove personally identifying content where possible.
  • Records required by legal order: if we are legally required to preserve specific data (e.g., subpoena, ongoing investigation), we may retain it until the legal hold is released.

Meta Platform deletion callback

For Meta's developer requirements, the technical deletion endpoint is:

  • https://kakoi.app/api/meta/data-deletion-callback

Meta uses this endpoint to notify Kakoi when a user revokes Kakoi from their Meta account or deletes their Meta account. On receiving such a notification, Kakoi automatically initiates the full deletion flow described in Option B above.
